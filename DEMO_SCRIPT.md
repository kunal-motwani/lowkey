# Flattr: 2-minute demo voiceover

**[0:00–0:15] Problem**
"We've all bought a shirt that looked perfect on the model, then looked tired in it. The colour bounces onto your face and amplifies what your skin is doing today. Personal-colour apps give you one static palette. Try-on shows fit. Nobody checks whether this shirt flatters *your* face *today*. So we built Flattr."

**[0:15–1:30] Live flow**
- "I take a selfie in natural light and pick an olive tee. One tap." *(click sample, run)*
- "The Stylist Agent starts. Step one runs two YouCam APIs in parallel: **AI Skin Analysis** reads today's dark circles, redness and radiance, and **AI Skin Tone Analysis** gets my skin colour and undertone."
- "Step two pulls the garment's dominant colour. Step three is our own Flattery engine: colour-theory rules that link shirt colour to a specific skin metric."
- "Score: low. And the reason is plain English: olive can throw a yellow-green cast under the eyes, and my dark circles are high today."
- "Below 70, so the agent plans fixes. First it ranks colourways for *my* skin and runs **AI Clothes Virtual Try-On** on the best one: here's the same tee in a colour that works on me."
- "Second, the rescue path. I love this shirt, so the agent picks a peach concealer, lip and blush, and **AI Makeup Virtual Try-On** applies them on top of the try-on. Shirt and makeup together."
- "Three looks side by side: original, better colour, rescue with makeup. The timeline shows every step and which YouCam API ran."

**[1:30–1:50] Impact**
"Colour mismatch is a hidden driver of fashion returns. Flattr can ship as a browser extension for shoppers and a widget or API for retailers, a per-shopper flattery score per SKU that cuts returns and lifts conversion."

**[1:50–2:00] Close**
"Flattr: will this make me look tired? Check before you buy."
