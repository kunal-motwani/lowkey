# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with care.
- Respect others' privacy. Do not post other people's photos or personal data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or unwelcome advances
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Comments that demean people for their skin, body or appearance

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code and may ban contributors for repeated or serious violations. Report concerns privately to the maintainers via the repository's contact options (see SECURITY.md for the private reporting channel). Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy applies in all project spaces.
